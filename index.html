1. Introduction and Acceptance

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") governs the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of information in connection with DavAI (the "Application"), a medication reminder and adherence-tracking application — covering any prescription across any medical specialty — developed and maintained by Arsh Duggal (the "Developer"). The Developer is based in India, and this Policy is intended to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDPA") and its rules, in addition to describing the Developer's practices generally. By downloading, installing, or using the Application, you ("User," "you") acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, you should not use the Application.

2. Definitions

  • "Application" means the DavAI mobile software application, in any version, distributed via the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person; this corresponds to "personal data" as defined under the DPDPA.
  • "Data Fiduciary" means the Developer, in its capacity as the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing Personal Data under the Application, as that term is used in the DPDPA. This is the same role referred to elsewhere in this Policy as "Data Controller."
  • "Data Principal" means the individual to whom the Personal Data relates, as that term is used in the DPDPA — referred to elsewhere in this Policy as the "User."
  • "Nominee" means a person a Data Principal may designate to exercise their rights under the DPDPA in the event of the Data Principal's death or incapacity, as described in Section 12.
  • "Profile" means a named person tracked within the Application on a given Device — either the Device owner ("Self Profile") or someone a User is caring for ("Dependent Profile"), as described in Section 7.
  • "Local Storage" means data storage located solely on the User's own Device, not transmitted to or accessible by the Developer.
  • "Third-Party Service Provider" means an independent third-party organization engaged to process specific data on the Developer's behalf, as further described in Section 6.
  • "Device" means the smartphone, tablet, or other hardware on which the Application is installed.

4. How We Use Information

Information described in Section 3 is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To display and personalize the Application interface for each profile on the Device;
  • To generate, schedule, and deliver medication reminder notifications;
  • To calculate and display adherence statistics, including completion percentage and streaks;
  • To detect and log a missed dose for a Dependent Profile, and to alert the User on this Device, as described in Section 7;
  • To extract structured medication information from a prescription photograph submitted by the User, via the Third-Party Service Provider described in Section 6, including flagging any reading the AI was not confident in so the User can verify it before relying on it.

Information is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build behavioral or marketing profiles. The Application does not track Users — including child Users, see Section 16 — across other apps or websites for any purpose.

6. Third-Party Service Providers and Disclosure

When the User submits a prescription photograph via the "Scan Prescription" feature, that image is sent from the User's Device to a relay service the Developer operates (a serverless function hosted on Cloudflare, Inc.), which forwards it to OpenAI, L.L.C. ("OpenAI") for the sole purpose of extracting structured medication data (name, form, dosage, frequency, and duration). The Developer's relay does not persist, log, or retain the photograph or the extracted data — it exists only to keep the Developer's OpenAI credentials off the User's Device, and the image passes through it transiently, in memory, for the duration of a single request. Separately, if a User is watched by a guardian, a missed-dose push notification is delivered via Apple Inc.'s Push Notification service (iOS guardians) or Google LLC's Firebase Cloud Messaging service (Android guardians), as described in Section 7.

This transmission constitutes the entirety of data disclosed by the Application to any third party. Per OpenAI's published API data usage terms, data submitted through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models by default, and is subject to OpenAI's own Privacy Policy, which governs OpenAI's independent processing of that data as a separate data controller for its own retention and security practices. Cloudflare processes the transient scan request, and the persistent guardian-sync dataset described in Section 8, as the Developer's infrastructure provider and is subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy in that capacity. Google processes an Android guardian Device's push token and the missed-dose alert content solely to deliver that notification, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. No API key or credential of any kind is stored on, or ever visible from, the User's Device. Users who wish to minimize the information contained in a submitted photograph may crop or redact portions of a document not necessary for medication extraction (e.g., full addresses or identification numbers) prior to submission.

Other than the disclosures described in this Section, the Developer does not sell, rent, lease, or otherwise disclose Personal Data to any advertiser, data broker, analytics provider, or other third party.

7. Guardian and Dependent Profiles

The Application lets a User set up their own Profile ("Self Profile") and, separately, lets another User on a different Device link as a guardian to watch that Self Profile — for example, an adult child watching a parent's medication schedule from their own phone. This is a cross-device feature: a Self Profile's current medication list and daily dose-taken status are synced to a server the Developer operates (a Cloudflare Workers application with a Cloudflare D1 database, both provided by Cloudflare, Inc.), so that a linked guardian's Device can display a live, read-only copy and receive a push notification if a dose goes 30 or more minutes unmarked past its scheduled time.

A pairing code is generated for each Self Profile. A guardian must enter that code — obtained directly from the person they're caring for — to link their Device; the code is validated against the Developer's server before linking succeeds. Once linked, the Self Profile's Device automatically syncs its current medication list (medicine name, dosage form, frequency, instructions, duration, and today's dose-taken status) to the server on every change. This synced copy does not include the User's name beyond what was entered for the Profile, adherence history, or any data from other Profiles on the same Device. A guardian's view is read-only: the Application does not allow a guardian to edit, delete, or mark doses taken on someone else's Profile.

To deliver a missed-dose push notification, a guardian's Device registers a platform push-notification token — issued by Apple for iOS Devices, or obtained via Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging service for Android Devices — with the Developer's server, associated only with the pairing code they're watching. The server checks, on a recurring automated schedule, whether any watched Profile has a dose that has gone unmarked 30 or more minutes past its scheduled time; if so, it sends the notification directly to Apple's Push Notification service or Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging service, as applicable to the guardian's Device, which delivers it. This check-and-alert log (which profile, medicine, and date — not the medication list itself) is retained on the server so the guardian's Application can display a short history of past alerts.

A User may remove a guardian link (or a Self Profile may stop syncing) at any time by removing the relevant Profile within the Application, which deletes that Profile's synced data and any registered push token from the Developer's server. A User who never sets up or is never linked to a guardian relationship has no data described in this Section transmitted to or stored on the Developer's server.

8. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

The full Personal Data described in Section 3 — every Profile's complete medication records, adherence history, and settings — is held in Local Storage on the User's Device only, using the standard application-storage mechanisms provided by the Device's operating system (iOS or Android). The Developer does not operate, and this Application does not transmit data to, any centralized database for that full dataset.

The exception is the narrower guardian-sync dataset described in Section 7: for a Self Profile that has at least one linked guardian, a reduced copy (current medication list and today's dose-taken status only — not adherence history, and not data from any other Profile on the Device) is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database the Developer operates, along with any guardian push notification tokens and a log of past missed-dose alerts for that Profile. This copy persists on the server for as long as the guardian link remains active, is overwritten on every sync (not accumulated as a history), and is deleted from the server when the Profile or the guardian link is removed within the Application, as described in Section 7.

Data held in Local Storage is retained for as long as the Application remains installed on the Device, or until the User deletes a specific Profile or record within the Application. Removing a Profile deletes that Profile's medications, reminders, and any related missed-dose log entries from the Device, and (where applicable) its synced copy and push token from the Developer's server. Uninstalling the Application permanently and irretrievably deletes all associated Local Storage data in accordance with the Device operating system's standard app-removal behavior; because a push token tied to the uninstalled Device also stops functioning, no further guardian alerts are sent for Profiles it was linked to, though the last-synced server-side copy may persist until separately removed or naturally superseded.

9. Data Security

The Application relies on the security architecture provided by the underlying mobile operating system (iOS or Android) to protect Local Storage, including operating-system-level application sandboxing that prevents other applications from accessing the Application's stored data without authorization. Transmission of a prescription photograph to the Third-Party Service Provider described in Section 6, and of the guardian-sync dataset described in Section 8, occurs over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection. While the Developer takes commercially reasonable steps consistent with these platform protections, and with the DPDPA's requirement to implement reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal data breach, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and the Developer cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Right to Nominate

Under the DPDPA, a Data Principal has the right to nominate another individual to exercise their rights under the Act on their behalf in the event of the Data Principal's death or incapacity. Because the Application does not operate an account or sign-in system, this nomination is handled outside the Application: a User who wishes to designate a Nominee may do so by writing to the Developer at the contact details in Section 23 with the Nominee's identity and contact information. On reasonable verification of the Nominee's identity and of the circumstances (the Data Principal's death or incapacity), the Developer will treat requests from that Nominee as if made by the Data Principal, solely with respect to the Personal Data described in this Policy.

This mechanism is distinct from, and not a substitute for, the Application's in-product Guardian feature described in Section 7. The Guardian feature is a live medication-monitoring feature a User sets up voluntarily while active and able to do so themselves, and linking a guardian does not itself grant that guardian any rights over the User's Personal Data or account under this Policy or the DPDPA.

15. International Users and Cross-Border Transfer

The Application may be downloaded and used by Users located in jurisdictions outside the Developer's home jurisdiction. Substantially all Personal Data is held in Local Storage on the User's own Device, with three exceptions: the transmission described in Section 6, processed by OpenAI; the guardian cross-device sync described in Section 7, processed by Cloudflare on its global network; and, for Android guardians, push delivery via Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging service — each in accordance with that provider's own privacy practices and applicable international data-transfer safeguards as described in its respective privacy policy (linked in Section 6).

Under the DPDPA's cross-border transfer regime, transfer of Personal Data outside India is permitted by default, except to a country or territory the Central Government of India has specifically restricted by notification. As of the Effective Date of this Policy, no such restriction applies to the jurisdictions in which OpenAI, Cloudflare, or Google process data as described in this Policy.

16. Children's Privacy

The DPDPA defines a "child" as an individual under the age of 18 and requires verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before processing a child's Personal Data, and separately prohibits processing that involves tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children. The Application does not track, behaviorally monitor for advertising purposes, or serve targeted advertising to any User of any age, so that prohibition is satisfied by the Application's design regardless of age.

The Application's Self Profile setup is intended for use by an individual capable of providing consent to this Policy in their own right, and the Developer does not knowingly permit a child under 18 to create a Self Profile and accept this Policy in their own name. A minor's medication information may instead be entered and managed by their parent or lawful guardian, acting as the User, via a Dependent Profile the parent or guardian themselves operates on their own Device — in which case the parent or guardian's own acceptance of this Policy, together with their exercise of parental or guardian authority in choosing to enter that minor's data, is the basis on which that data is processed. A parent or lawful guardian may withdraw consent at any time by removing the relevant Profile, which deletes that minor's data as described in Section 8. If a parent or lawful guardian believes a child has independently created a Self Profile and provided Personal Data about themselves, they may contact the Developer using the details in Section 23 to request deletion; because data is held in Local Storage, deletion may also be accomplished directly by removing the relevant Profile or uninstalling the Application.
