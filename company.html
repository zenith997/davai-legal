DavAI

Medication reminders and adherence tracking for any prescription — with a guardian who's alerted if a dose is missed.

What DavAI does

DavAI helps people track and remember their medications — tablets, capsules, syrups, injections, drops, inhalers, creams, patches, and more — across every medical specialty, not just one. Prescriptions can be scanned with AI or entered manually, and reminders are scheduled automatically so nothing gets missed.

Its guardian feature lets a family member or caregiver link to someone else's medication schedule with a simple pairing code, getting a live read-only view and a notification if a dose goes unmarked 30 or more minutes past its scheduled time — without either person needing an account or sign-in.
