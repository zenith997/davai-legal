Deleting Your Data

Self-service (fastest): open DavAI, go to Settings, and remove the profile you want to delete. This immediately deletes that profile's medications from your device, and — if it was ever linked to a guardian — deletes its synced copy (medication list, today's dose status, and any registered push tokens) from the server. This works without needing to contact us or wait for a response.

By request: if you'd rather have us do it, or want confirmation a deletion completed, email davai.pvt.ltd@gmail.com with the pairing code or profile name involved. We'll acknowledge the request and delete the corresponding server-side data.

See the Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion section of our Privacy Policy for full details on what's stored where and for how long.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I mark a dose as taken?
Doses are time-locked to keep your adherence history accurate — you can't mark a dose taken before its scheduled time. The reminder card shows you exactly when it unlocks.

Where is my data stored?
On your device by default. If you link a guardian, your current medication list and today's dose status also sync to DavAI's server so the guardian's device can show it live and alert them about a missed dose — nothing else syncs, and there's still no account or sign-in. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How does the Guardian feature work?
Set up a profile for yourself, then share its pairing code with someone you'd like as a guardian. On their own, separate device, they choose "I'm caring for someone else" and enter that code to get a live, read-only view of your medications and a notification if a dose goes unmarked 30+ minutes past its scheduled time. A guardian can never edit, delete, or mark a dose taken on your profile.

Why does a "missed dose" alert show up for someone I'm caring for?
If a dependent's dose is still unmarked 30+ minutes after its scheduled time, DavAI's server detects it and sends a push notification straight to your device — you don't need the app open, and neither does the person you're caring for.

The AI scanner misread my prescription — what do I do?
Tap any medication in the review screen to correct the name, form, dosage, frequency, or duration before saving. Medications the scanner wasn't confident about are flagged with a warning so you know to double-check them against the original prescription. You can also add medications manually from the same screen.

Why did scanning fail with a "daily limit reached" message?
To keep the scanning service sustainable, each profile can scan up to 5 prescriptions per day. The limit resets the next day, or you can add a medication manually in the meantime.

Notifications aren't showing up.
Check that notifications are allowed for DavAI in your device's Settings app, and that Do Not Disturb / Focus modes aren't silencing them.
