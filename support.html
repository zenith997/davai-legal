Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I mark a dose as taken?
Doses are time-locked to keep your adherence history accurate — you can't mark a dose taken before its scheduled time. The reminder card shows you exactly when it unlocks.

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. DavAI doesn't have user accounts or cloud sync. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How does the Guardian feature work?
You can add a profile for someone you're caring for and manage their medications directly from your own phone — that's the device that gets reminders and missed-dose alerts for them. Live syncing between two separate devices (e.g. so a dependent's own phone can notify you) isn't available yet; today the pairing code is a label reserved for that future version.

Why does a "missed dose" alert show up for someone I'm caring for?
If a dependent's dose is still unmarked a while after its scheduled time, DavAI logs it in the Guardian tab and sends a notification on your device, since that's where you're tracking their medications.

The AI scanner misread my prescription — what do I do?
Tap any medication in the review screen to correct the name, form, dosage, frequency, or duration before saving. You can also add medications manually from the same screen.

The scanner says my API key is missing or invalid.
Prescription scanning uses your own OpenAI API key, entered in Settings. Double-check the key is correct and has available quota on your OpenAI account. You can always add medications manually instead.

Notifications aren't showing up.
Check that notifications are allowed for DavAI in your device's Settings app, and that Do Not Disturb / Focus modes aren't silencing them.
